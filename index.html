    Lost in Space

    A rover has just landed on a planet nobody has mapped. It has 380 Wh of battery, 80 MB of storage and one job: bring back as much science as it can, then get home before the power runs out.

    • It starts blind. The mast camera sees about 60 m, and hills block the view.
    • Data only counts once it's sent. Samples have to be uplinked from a relay zone. Anything still on board is lost if the rover strands.
    • The planet fights back. Rockslides bury known routes, dust storms roll in and relays drop offline. You can trigger them yourself.

    Space plays and pauses. N steps one tick. Keys 1, 2 and 3 pick a hazard to drop.
